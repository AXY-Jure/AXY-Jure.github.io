Questions

Back Office, briefly answered.

What can be managed in AXY Back Office?+

Back Office is designed to manage products and catalogues, stock context, orders, invoices, customers, business units, company profiles, partners, media, announcements and management analysis. The exact modules and editable fields depend on the configured workspace and source systems.

How do I add products from a partner?+

When a connected brand makes an approved catalogue available, your company can review and accept the relevant selection. Supported fields are organised into the AXY product structure according to the configured mapping and permissions.

Do I need a custom integration for every manufacturer?+

AXY is designed to provide a shared connection layer for supported partner workflows, reducing separate point-to-point work. Each partner and source system still requires available data, mapping, permissions and an agreed implementation scope.

Does Back Office replace our ERP, CRM, PIM or accounting system?+

Not necessarily. During setup, each record type is assigned an authoritative source. AXY can manage selected workflows directly or exchange approved data with the systems that remain the source of truth.

Does every update happen automatically?+

No. Product and partner updates can be reviewed before acceptance. Automatic synchronisation is used only when the integration, update rule and permission model have been deliberately configured.

Can a partner see our customers or internal records?+

Not by default. A partner receives only the fields and actions required for an approved workflow. Customer identities, internal notes, margins and commercial strategy remain private unless a deliberate permission says otherwise.
