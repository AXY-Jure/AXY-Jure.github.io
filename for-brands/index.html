Questions from brands and manufacturers

What commercial and technology teams usually ask.

What can a brand learn from activity in stores?+

Where participating retailers capture and approve the relevant workflow, AXY can combine signals such as products shown, compared, wishlisted, requested or unavailable with connected stock and sell-through data. The exact view depends on available data, permissions and sample size.

Does AXY expose retailer customer data to brands?+

No—not by default. Retailer customer identities and internal records remain private unless a specific, permitted workflow requires defined fields to be shared. Broader product and market insight can use aggregated or anonymised signals.

How do brands keep retailer product information current?+

Brands can publish approved product records, collections, images, specifications, documents and commercial fields to connected authorised retailers. Retailers review and accept the products or updates relevant to their business unless an approved synchronisation rule has been configured.

Can AXY connect with an ERP, PIM or order system?+

Yes, where the source provides suitable access. AXY can use APIs, scheduled synchronisation, structured imports or a scoped adapter. The records, direction, frequency and automation are confirmed during implementation.
