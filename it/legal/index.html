Introductory provision

This Privacy Policy on the Protection of Personal Data is applied by the company XY Sales d.o.o., Zagreb, Jordanovac 60, OIB: 31868998641 in connection with the AXY platform and its applications, including the AXY Back Office, AXY Sales App and AXY Customer App (together, the “Application”/ “AXY”).

AXY is a freemium B2B software platform for retail businesses, brands and their authorized employees. It connects product information, in-store sales activity, customers, stock visibility, follow-up, warranties, partner collaboration and business communication in one operating environment, with the purpose to enhance the customer experience.

The Application is used by different categories of users. AXY business customers are businesses or organisations that administer an organisation account. Their employees and other authorised internal users may use the AXY Sales App and related business functionality. Individual end customers may use the consumer-facing AXY Customer App.

The nature and scope of personal data processing therefore depend on the relevant application, user category, enabled modules and the relationship between the data subject, AXY and the participating business.

AXY Applications and User Categories

Application Primary users Description
AXY Sales App Employees / Authorized Users The business-facing mobile application intended for authorized employees and other internal users of AXY business customers. Depending on their assigned roles, permissions and enabled functionalities, users may manage customer-facing activities, including product presentation and scanning, customer and product interactions, customer follow-up, tasks, stock visibility, warranty-related workflows, messaging and other business activities supported by AXY.
AXY Customer App (“Customer App”) Customer App Users The consumer-facing mobile application intended for individual end customers. It enables users, depending on the available functionalities, to view products and visits shared with them, save products to wishlists, continue their shopping journey, manage service and warranty interactions, communicate with participating businesses and manage the information they choose to share through the Application.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • “Employee” or “Authorized User” means a named internal user authorized by an AXY business customer to access and use the AXY Sales App or other business-facing AXY functionalities.
  • “Customer App User” means an individual end customer using the AXY Customer App and is a separate category of user from an Employee or Authorized User.
  • “User”, where used generally in this Privacy Policy, means an Employee / Authorized User or a Customer App User, as applicable.

The nature and scope of personal data processed by AXY may differ depending on the relevant Application, the category of User, the functionalities and modules used and the User’s relationship with AXY and the relevant participating business.

With this Privacy Policy, AXY wishes to provide a complete insight into the method of collecting and processing Personal Data when using the Application and to point out the care taken to protect personal data collected through the Application itself.

Where AXY determines the purposes and means of processing personal data for its own purposes, including the administration, security, analytics and development of the Application, AXY acts as the data controller.

Where AXY processes personal data entered into or otherwise made available through the Application by a participating business on behalf of that business and solely for the purpose of providing the relevant Application functionalities in accordance with that business’s instructions, AXY generally acts as the data processor, while the participating business acts as the data controller.

In the part of the Application use process when AXY acts as a processor on the instructions of the User as the data controller, AXY shall act in accordance with all applicable personal data protection standards, including concluding appropriate Personal Data Processing Agreements with data controllers.

By taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing as well as the risk of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons, AXY shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

Storage period

AXY retains personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking into account the relevant Application, the User category, the enabled functionality or module, the duration of the relevant account or business relationship, applicable statutory retention requirements and the need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

As a general rule, the following retention criteria apply:

  • account, profile and access data relating to Employees / Authorized Users are retained while the relevant user remains authorized under the Customer’s account. Where employment or authorization changes, the Customer is responsible for updating, suspending or removing access. Following deactivation, personal data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer required for account administration, security, audit, compliance or legal claims;
  • the AXY account and all related personal data under AXY’s control are retained while the Customer App account is active. Where a Customer App User selects “Delete account” through the available in-app account-deletion functionality, the User will be required to verify their identity using their password or a one-time password (OTP) and submit a request for permanent deletion of the entire AXY account and all related personal data under AXY’s control. AXY will then manually process the deletion request within one business day and, upon completion, send a confirmation to the User by e-mail. As an exception, AXY may retain personal data where and for as long as retention is required or permitted for invoicing, accounting or other statutory obligations, warranty or contractual relationships, or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims;
  • wishlist, product-view, click, visit, shopping-journey, customer-follow-up and similar interaction data are retained for as long as they remain relevant to the active customer relationship and the functionality for which they were collected. Such data will be deleted or anonymised when the relevant account or relationship ends or when it is no longer necessary for those purposes, subject to any applicable legal retention requirement;
  • messages, photographs, attachments, notes and other user-provided or communication content are retained for as long as necessary to provide the relevant messaging, customer, service or business workflow and, where applicable, for a limited period thereafter where necessary for security, dispute resolution or legal claims. Where messaging is provided through an external communications provider, that provider may apply its own retention periods in accordance with its applicable terms and privacy information;
  • warranty and service data are retained for the duration of the relevant warranty or service relationship and thereafter for the period necessary to handle claims, demonstrate performance of the relevant service or comply with applicable legal obligations. Where data is shared with a manufacturer or warranty provider for a warranty extension, that recipient may retain the data in accordance with its own legal obligations and applicable privacy information;
  • technical, security, audit and system-log data are retained for a period appropriate to the relevant security, troubleshooting, fraud-prevention, audit or system-integrity purpose and are deleted or anonymised once they are no longer necessary for that purpose, unless a longer period is required in connection with a security incident or legal claim;
  • analytics, benchmarking and product-development data are retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant analysis, measurement, improvement or development purpose. Wherever reasonably possible, AXY will use aggregated, pseudonymised or anonymised data for these purposes. Data that has been irreversibly anonymised so that it no longer relates to an identified or identifiable individual is no longer personal data and may be retained for statistical, benchmarking or product-development purposes;
  • personal data processed solely on the basis of consent will no longer be processed for the consent-based purpose after consent is withdrawn, unless another legal basis permits or requires continued retention;
  • accounting, tax, employment and other records subject to statutory retention requirements are retained for the periods prescribed by applicable law.

Upon termination, suspension or downgrade of an account or a paid module, the availability, export, retention and deletion of data may depend on the affected functionality and the applicable contractual arrangements. Personal data will not be retained merely because a module or account is no longer active; AXY will retain it only where a continuing lawful purpose or legal obligation applies.

At the end of the applicable retention period, personal data will be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise placed beyond use in accordance with AXY’s deletion procedures. Because the exact retention period may depend on the type of data, the relevant Application or module and applicable legal requirements, further information about a specific retention period may be requested using the privacy contact details below.

Data subject’s rights

(ii) RIGHT TO RECTIFICATION OF PERSONAL DATA – the data subject may ask AXY to correct or modify personal data at any time in writing to AXY or electronically to the e-mail address: jure@axy.net.

(iii) RIGHT TO ERASURE OF PERSONAL DATA – the data subject may ask AXY to erase personal data in writing to the AXY address or electronically to the e-mail address: jure@axy.net. AXY shall erase personal data without undue delay where the conditions in Article 17 of the GDPR are met, subject to the exceptions provided by applicable law.

AXY shall delete the mentioned data without undue delay if one of the following conditions is met:

  • the personal data are no longer necessary in relation to the purposes for which they were collected or otherwise processed;
  • the data subject has withdrawn the consent on which the processing is based and there is no other legal basis for the processing;
  • the data subject has objected to the processing in accordance with Article 21(1) of the Regulation and there are no overriding legitimate grounds for the processing;
  • the data subject has objected to the processing in accordance with Article 21(2) of the Regulation;
  • the personal data are unlawfully processed;
  • the personal data must be deleted for compliance with a legal obligation under applicable law

Personal data shall not be deleted if they are necessary for the resolution of an objection or are required for the establishment or defense of legal claims in judicial, arbitration, administrative or other equivalent proceedings. In such cases, personal data will be stored until the successful completion of the proceedings.

(iv) RIGHT TO RESTRICT PROCESSING – the data subject may request restriction of processing in the circumstances provided by Article 18 of the GDPR, in writing to AXY or electronically to the e-mail address: jure@axy.net.

(v) RIGHT TO DATA PORTABILITY – where the conditions in Article 20 of the GDPR are met, the data subject may request personal data provided to AXY in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and may request transmission of such data to another controller where technically feasible.

(vi) RIGHT TO OBJECT – where processing is based on Article 6(1)(e) or (f) of the GDPR, the data subject may object to such processing on grounds relating to their particular situation. The data subject has the right to object at any time to processing for direct marketing purposes, including profiling related to such direct marketing.

(vii) THE RIGHT TO FILE A COMPLAINT WITH THE SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY – the data subject has the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of their habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement, if they consider that the processing of their personal data infringes the GDPR.

Where AXY introduces processing involving solely automated decision-making that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects a data subject within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR, AXY will provide the information and safeguards required by applicable law, including the right to obtain human intervention where applicable.
