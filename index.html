Fits your stack

Standalone first. Connected when you’re ready.

AXY can run on its own or connect your POS, ERP, CRM, inventory and messaging tools. Your existing systems stay where they are.

Who owns the data?

Each business owns its own data. Retailers own store and customer activity. Brands own their product content. Customers control their personal information.

What requires approval?

Any visibility between partners. Sharing is opt-in, and each retailer decides which signals a brand may see.
