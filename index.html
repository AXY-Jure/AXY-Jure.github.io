The operating model

Capture. Continue. Understand.

Three connected stages, one shared context.

1 · CAPTURE
Record the work as it happens

Store visits, products shown, customer interest, tasks, inquiries and service intake — captured in seconds during normal work, not typed up afterwards.

2 · CONTINUE
Keep the relationship moving

Follow-ups, Wishlist's, offers, messages, warranty and service updates in the clients pocket. The visit doesn’t end when the customer walks out.

3 · UNDERSTAND
See what the activity means

Captured interactions reveal what customers want, which products perform, and where opportunities are being missed — giving teams clear suggestions on what to show, follow up on, stock, or order next.
